Arauco Chain

Arauco Chain is a Blockchain network dedicated to enable payments for everyone and to be interoperable with other financial networks.

What is?

Arauco Chain is an enterprise-grade and global Open Finance network (Blockchain) to connect Web 3.0 with Fintechs and regulated financial institutions.

Why Arauco Chain

  1. Permissionless and complaint: FinTechs and financial institutions can build in a network compliant with various local and international regulations.
  2. Integrated interoperability: Using modular bridging, connect to any financial network to send any asset, including other blockchains and non-blockchain networks.
  3. AgentFi: Build virtual agents and AI wallets with our high-performance network to operate financial services on your behalf (AgentFi).

  • Learn more

Integrated interoperability

Arauco Chain is a hub for different financial networks, so users, Fintechs, and financial institutions can operate on any network (Blockchain and non Blockchain) transparently and securely.
Some of the networks include TON, Solana, and Base, among others, to move assets without major technical difficulty.

Smart Currencies

Smart Currencies is an Arauco Chain module that allows builders to create different types of tokens (stablecoins, CBDCs, RWA, among others) with different attributes that are compatible with regulations in different countries.

Fintech Functions

Arauco Chain will natively enable financial functions (Fintech functions) so that Fintechs, DeFintechs, and Decentralized Apps can create and use these components in services associated with transactions and payments.

Central Limit Order Book

An on-chain Central Limit Order Book (CLOB) offers several significant benefits compared to centralized order books and liquidity pools on decentralized exchanges (DEXs). Arauco Chain will natively incorporate one.
